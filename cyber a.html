🛡️ Women Online Safety

Women should be careful while sharing personal information and communicating with unknown people online.

Safety Tips

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Do not share OTP or passwords.
  • Avoid clicking unknown links.
  • Keep social media accounts private.
  • Save screenshots if you face an online problem.

📱 Women Social Media Safety

Protect your social media account and personal information from unknown people and suspicious accounts.

Safety Tips

  • Do not accept unknown friend requests.
  • Use a strong and unique password.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Block suspicious accounts.
  • Report abusive or fake accounts.

👤 Women - Fake Profile

A fake profile may use a person's name, photograph or information without permission.

Safety Steps

  • Take screenshots.
  • Report the fake profile.
  • Block the account if necessary.
  • Keep evidence safely.

🎣 Phishing Attack

Phishing uses fake messages, emails or websites to trick people into sharing information.

Safety Steps

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Keep screenshots as evidence.

📱 OTP / UPI Fraud

Fraudsters may request payment information by pretending to be trusted people or services.

Safety Steps

  • Never share OTP or PIN.
  • Do not approve unknown payment requests.
  • Check payment details carefully.
  • Contact your bank for unauthorized transactions.

📸 Social Media Impersonation

A fake account may be created using another person's name or photographs.

Safety Steps

  • Take screenshots.
  • Report the fake profile.
  • Block the account.
  • Keep evidence.

🕵️ Cyberstalking

Cyberstalking can involve repeated unwanted messages or online monitoring.

Safety Steps

  • Do not engage with threatening messages.
  • Block unwanted accounts.
  • Save evidence.
  • Tell a trusted person.

🔓 Account Hacking

Unauthorized access to an email or social media account can create a cyber security problem.

Safety Steps

  • Change the password.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Review active sessions.
  • Secure recovery details.

💳 Online Financial Fraud

Online financial fraud may include unauthorized transactions and deceptive payment requests.

Safety Steps

  • Do not share OTP or PIN.
  • Contact your bank.
  • Keep transaction details.
  • Report through appropriate channels.
